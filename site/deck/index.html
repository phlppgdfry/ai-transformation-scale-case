Our bet: win on fresh

Fresh is a third of sales and the reason customers choose Ardena. Competitors automate ordering and personalise offers; nobody visibly invests in fresh.

  • Build what differentiates: fresh markdowns, then store ordering.
  • Catch up on personalisation, Ardena's way: offers that know what is fresh in your store.
  • Buy the commodity cheaply: office, code and service assistants.

The first choice is robust

93%

of 2,000 random re-weightings of your own criteria keep the fresh markdown advisor in the top five.

It works in stores

−18%

fresh waste in 12 pilot stores against their matched controls (0.88 pp), margin up, availability intact, 79% of suggestions accepted by staff.

Without control stores we would have reported 0.62 pp: warm weeks raised waste everywhere.

It is not ready for 240 stores yet

5 blocking gates are open: an automated data feed, monitoring, retraining, a support model and a year-1 budget. None of them is about the model.

Roll-out in three waves from month 7; each wave can stop the next.
