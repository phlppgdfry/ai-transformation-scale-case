# Talk outline: "From 37 pilots to one in production"

*20 minutes for a retail or operations audience. Slides: one message each.*

| Min | Slide message | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | "AI everywhere and nowhere" is the normal state | The CEO's quote; 37 initiatives, 3 in production |
| 2–5 | Most initiatives fail on ownership and measurement, not on models | Triage: 11 measured, 14 to fix |
| 5–8 | Decide the criteria before the candidates | Robustness: the first choice holds in 93% of weightings |
| 8–11 | Separate commodity from differentiation | Two tracks; the quick wins no longer crowd out the bets |
| 11–15 | Prove it with a comparison group | Pilot: 18% less fresh waste; before/after would have under-reported it by a third |
| 15–18 | Production is gates, not a date | 5 blocking gates; none of them about the model |
| 18–20 | Three numbers to track | Spend with an owner, time to decision, initiatives stopped |
