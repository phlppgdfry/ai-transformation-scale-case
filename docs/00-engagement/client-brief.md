# Client brief: Ardena Group

> Fictional client. Ardena Group, its people, stores, competitors and figures are invented for this case.

## The client

Ardena Group is a Belgian grocery retailer: 240 supermarkets, three distribution centres, home delivery and click & collect, about 11,000 employees and €3.6bn revenue at a 2.8% EBIT margin. Fresh products are a third of sales and the main reason customers choose Ardena, and the main source of waste.

## The ask, from the CEO

> "We have AI everywhere and nowhere. Every department has a pilot, some people pay for tools on their own credit card, and I cannot name one AI initiative that changed our P&L. Our competitors are automating ordering and personalising offers. I want to know which bets to make, how to organise ourselves, and I want one thing in production that proves it, within six months."

That contains three asks, which map onto the three parts of the engagement:

| The CEO said | What it means | Engagement track |
|---|---|---|
| "AI everywhere and nowhere", "own credit card" | Scattered efforts, shadow AI, no governance, no way to stop things | **Organisational AI readiness**: maturity, initiative triage, governance and an orchestration layer |
| "Which bets to make", "competitors are …" | A prioritised portfolio and a view on where AI creates an advantage | **AI discovery**: workshop series, growth map, differentiation strategy, roadmap |
| "One thing in production that proves it" | A pilot with measured business impact and a real path to scale | **Applied AI**: pilot charter, evaluation, pilot-to-production plan |

## How we work

The engagement runs for 22 weeks with two people embedded at Ardena's head office in Mechelen three days a week, plus a pilot squad. We are part of the client's weekly rhythm (executive committee, IT steering, store operations meeting), not a team that visits for steering committees. See the [engagement plan](engagement-plan.md).

## What success looks like at week 22

1. The executive committee has approved a ranked AI portfolio and an 18-month roadmap with owners, budgets and KPIs.
2. Every existing AI initiative has a decision (scale, fix, merge, pause or stop) and an owner for that decision.
3. An AI policy, an AI council and an intake process are live, and the first systems are in an AI register.
4. The pilot has a measured outcome against pre-registered criteria, and a gated plan to production.
5. Ardena can run the next prioritisation round without us (toolkit and playbook handed over).

## One expectation we reset in week 1

The CEO asked for "one thing in production within six months". We agreed instead on **one thing proven in stores with a measured outcome, and a gated plan to production**. Putting a pilot into 240 stores before the data feed, monitoring and support exist is how pilots die after launch. The [pilot-to-production gates](../04-applied-ai/pilot-to-production.md) show what was still missing at the end of the pilot.
