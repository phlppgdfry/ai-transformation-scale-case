# Ardena AI policy (one page)

*For every employee. Approved by the executive committee. Owner: AI office.*

**Why.** AI can save us time and help us serve customers better. Used carelessly, it can leak data, treat people unfairly or give wrong answers that we then pass on. This page tells you how to use AI at Ardena.

**1. Use the approved tools.** The AI office keeps a list of approved tools on the intranet. Use those for work. Do not put Ardena data in personal accounts or free online tools.

**2. Protect data.** Never put customer data, colleagues' personal data, passwords, contracts or source code into a tool that is not approved for that kind of data. When in doubt, ask the AI office.

**3. You stay responsible.** AI suggests; you decide. Check what AI produces before you use it, especially numbers, prices, legal or HR content, and anything you send to a customer.

**4. Be open about AI.** Tell customers when they talk to an AI. Label AI-generated images and content where they could be mistaken for real.

**5. People decisions are made by people.** At Ardena, AI does not decide on hiring, promotion, scheduling, discipline or dismissal. It may help prepare information; a person decides and can explain why.

**6. New AI use goes through intake.** Want to try a new AI tool or build something? Fill in the intake form. Simple, low-risk requests are answered within five working days.

**7. Report problems.** If AI gives a harmful, biased or strange result, or you think data leaked, report it to the service desk as an AI incident.

**8. Learn.** Everyone who uses AI at work completes the AI literacy module (30 minutes). Champions in your team can help.
