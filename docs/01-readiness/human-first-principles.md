# Human-first AI principles

Agreed with the executive committee and shared with the works council. They turn "responsible AI" into decisions that show up in the portfolio.

| Principle | What it means at Ardena | Where it changed the plan |
|---|---|---|
| **People decide what matters to people** | No AI decision on hiring, scheduling, performance, discipline or dismissal | CV screening (UC-22) parked; staff scheduling (UC-08) only after AI Act and works-council review, and without individual performance data |
| **Assist the expert, do not replace their judgement** | Suggestions with reasons; staff can override and their overrides improve the system | Markdown advisor: store staff confirm every markdown; acceptance rate is a pilot KPI |
| **No surveillance of customers or staff** | No biometric identification; no individual monitoring of staff productivity | Loss-prevention video analytics (UC-19) parked pending assessment |
| **Say when it is AI** | Customers know when they talk to an AI or see AI-generated content | Transparency duties on the 8 use cases in that tier |
| **Measure the effect on people, not just on cost** | Each use case tracks a people metric (time saved, workload, satisfaction) next to its business KPI | KPI framework |
| **Skills come with the tools** | No rollout without training; literacy for everyone who uses AI | F5 is a foundation, funded centrally |
