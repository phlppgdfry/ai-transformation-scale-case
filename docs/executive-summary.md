**Where AI makes Ardena harder to compete with.** Fresh. It is a third of sales, the reason customers choose Ardena, and competitors are not investing there. Build on fresh (markdowns, then store ordering); catch up on personalisation in Ardena's own way; buy the commodity tools cheaply.

**What we recommend.**
1. Fund five foundations centrally: governance, literacy, an AI orchestration layer, the retail data platform and master-data quality (<!--n:val.foundations_one_off-->€1.0m<!--/n--> one-off, <!--n:val.foundations_run-->€460k<!--/n--> a year).
2. Run two tracks: three squads build what differentiates; IT and the business adopt commodity tools in parallel.
3. Roll out the markdown advisor in three waves once the five blocking production gates are closed (<!--n:rm.uc01-->months 7–10<!--/n-->).
4. Measure every use case on a pre-registered outcome KPI, and stop what does not deliver.

**What it is worth.** Portfolio NPV of <!--n:val.portfolio_npv-->€12.5m<!--/n--> including the foundations, after a 30% haircut on workshop estimates. Illustrative; every assumption has an owner.

**What we will not do.** No AI decisions about people (hiring, scheduling, performance). No customer surveillance. No dynamic shelf pricing race with discounters.
