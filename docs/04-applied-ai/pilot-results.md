## Why the control group mattered

Warm weather in the second half of the pilot pushed waste up in **all** stores. A simple before/after on the pilot stores shows a reduction of only <!--n:pilot.naive-->0.62 pp<!--/n-->: it would have under-reported the effect by about a third and might have missed the 0.5 pp threshold with a little less luck. In other pilots the same mistake goes the other way and over-reports. Without a comparison group, a pilot measures the season as much as the solution.

## What the pilot does not prove

- **Assignment:** within each matched pair, the pilot store was the one whose manager volunteered. Assignment was not random, so part of the effect could come from motivated managers. Wave 1 assigns stores at random within pairs.
- **Scale:** the other 228 stores include weaker fresh teams. Wave 1 (60 stores) is the test.
- **Durability:** ten weeks. Benefit tracking continues for a year.
- **Production:** the pilot ran on a hand-built data extract and a squad on call. See [pilot to production](pilot-to-production.md).
