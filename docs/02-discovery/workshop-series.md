# Workshop series

Six workshops over seven weeks. Each one has a single output that the next one needs. The same format works for a hospital, a ministry or a manufacturer; only the value streams change.

| # | Workshop | Who | Duration | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ambition and value streams | ExCo | 3 h | AI ambition in three measurable outcomes; value streams to explore; scoring criteria and weights agreed |
| 2 | Use-case ideation: stores and fresh | COO stores, 6 store managers, fresh, HR | 3 h | 9 use-case canvases |
| 3 | Use-case ideation: supply and e-commerce | DC ops, transport, e-com, marketing | 3 h | 8 canvases |
| 4 | Use-case ideation: head office | Finance, HR, legal, IT, customer service | 3 h | 9 canvases |
| 5 | Scoring and growth map | ExCo + BU leads | 3 h | Scored portfolio, growth map, robustness discussed |
| 6 | Roadmap and owners | ExCo + AI council | 3 h | Roadmap, owner per use case, KPIs, budget request |

## Workshop 1: ambition and value streams (agenda)

| Time | Block | Method |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Why we are here; what AI is and is not (15 min, no hype) | Short talk with three Ardena examples |
| 0:15 | Where does value leak today? | Silent writing, then 1-2-4-all, per value stream (buy, move, sell, serve, support) |
| 0:55 | Three outcomes for AI by 2028 | Dot-voting, then wording in measurable terms |
| 1:30 | Break | |
| 1:45 | What would competitors do with AI? | Pre-read of the competitor scan; "if we were them" |
| 2:15 | Scoring criteria and their weights | Proposal on the wall; discuss; agree |
| 2:45 | What we will not do | Explicit list (for example: no AI decisions about people) |
| 2:55 | Close: what happens next | |

## Ideation workshops 2–4

- **Before:** every participant gets a pre-read with two pages: what AI can do in plain words, and five examples from retail (not only Ardena's competitors).
- **Start from the work, not from AI:** each group maps one process they know (ordering fresh, picking, answering a complaint) and marks the pains: waiting, rework, guessing, copying.
- **Then ask:** where would a prediction, a draft, a classification or a search help? Each idea becomes a [use-case canvas](../05-go-to-market/toolkit.md#use-case-canvas).
- **Sort out the noise early:** three questions per idea, on the spot: Who owns it? What data does it need, and do we have it? How would we know it worked?

## Workshop 5: scoring

Scoring is done in the room, with the criteria from workshop 1, and the result is immediately shown as a growth map. We then show the robustness test (2,000 random re-weightings): the ExCo sees which choices are robust and which depend on a particular weighting. That turns "my use case scored lower" into a discussion about the weights, which is where the discussion belongs.

## What worked and what we would change

- Store managers in workshop 2 produced the highest-value ideas. Getting six of them out of their stores for three hours needed the COO's personal invitation.
- Head office ideas were mostly productivity tools (commodity). Useful, but they should not take build capacity: that led to the separate adoption track in the roadmap.
- Next time: run the competitor scan **before** workshop 1, so the differentiation question shapes the ideation instead of being added afterwards.
