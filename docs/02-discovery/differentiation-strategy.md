# AI differentiation strategy

The question is not "where can Ardena use AI?" but **where can AI make Ardena harder to compete with**, and where should it simply keep up, as cheaply as possible.

## What competitors are doing (public signals, fictional)

| Competitor | Position | AI moves |
|---|---|---|
| Volta Markt | Discount, 310 stores | Fully automated store ordering; electronic shelf labels with dynamic pricing in 2027; minimal personalisation |
| Grenvale | Premium, strong e-commerce | Personalised offers in the app (2025); recipe and meal-planning assistant; delivery-slot pricing |
| BuurtBox | Quick commerce, city centres | Dark-store picking automation; neighbourhood demand sensing |

**What it means:** automated ordering is becoming table stakes in discount (parity for Ardena within two years); personalisation is where Grenvale leads; nobody visibly competes on **fresh**, which is a third of Ardena's sales and its main reason for customer choice.

## Ardena's AI bets

1. **Win on fresh.** Fresh markdowns (UC-01) first, then store-level ordering (UC-02) for fresh, using Ardena's own store, waste and supplier data. This is where Ardena's data is unique and competitors are not visibly investing. *Build.*
2. **Catch up on personalisation, differently.** Grenvale leads on offers; Ardena can combine loyalty data with fresh availability ("what is fresh near you today") instead of copying generic coupons. *Build on a configured platform.*
3. **Make the commodity cheap.** Office assistants, code assistants, invoice processing, energy optimisation: buy, standardise on one tool per need, negotiate centrally, run through the adoption track. They pay back but do not differentiate (together <!--n:val.commodity_npv-->€2.0m<!--/n--> NPV over three years).

## What Ardena will not try to win on

- **Dynamic shelf pricing:** Volta Markt is ahead, the hardware investment is large, and Ardena's customers value price stability. Revisit in 2028.
- **Quick-commerce automation:** not Ardena's model.

## The test for any new AI idea

Ask in this order: Does it use data only Ardena has? Does it improve something customers choose Ardena for (fresh, service, convenience)? Would a competitor need more than a year to copy it? Three yes: build. One or none: buy or configure, or do not do it.
